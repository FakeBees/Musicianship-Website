## 1. Your role at a glance

You're a **Section Teacher**. A School Admin has assigned you to teach one or more sections. You look
after the students in them, and you can shape each section's curriculum: hide course work it doesn't
need, and add modules and exercises of its own.

| You can | Ask your School Admin to |
|---|---|
| See every section assigned to you | Create or delete a section |
| Open a section's roster and see students' average scores | Change which course a section follows |
| See and share both join codes — the school's and the section's | Assign a teacher to a section |
| Remove a student from your section, or from your school | Change the course itself for every section |
| Rename your section | Add people to the school, or change anyone's role |
| Hide or restore the course's modules and exercises for your section | |
| Add modules and exercises for your section only | |
| Preview the site as a student | |

**How you got this role.** Your School Admin gave you the Section Teacher role in your school, and
your account picked it up automatically. If that role is ever removed, your account goes back to
being a student account.

## 2. Key ideas

**School.** Your organisation. People join it, and it owns the courses.

**Course.** A curriculum, such as *Theory 1*. A course is made of modules, and many sections can
follow the same course.

**Module.** A unit within a course, such as *Intervals*. Modules appear in a fixed order.

**Assignment.** One task inside a module. Each has a completion rule — either a number of
exercises, or a number of passes at a minimum score.

**Section.** A group of students taught together, usually named like a class section, such as
*MUS201-002*. **Your students see the word "Classroom" instead of "Section" — it's the same thing.**
Each section follows one course, and that course gives its students their modules.

**Your section's curriculum** is the course's modules and exercises, **plus** anything you add for your
section, **minus** anything you hide. Changes you make here affect only your section — never the course,
and never any other section following it.

**Join codes.** Short codes students type to join. There are two — a **school join code** and a
**section join code** — and students need both, in that order.

**Exercise types.** Melodic, Rhythmic, Harmonic and Holistic dictation.

```
School
 └─ Course               e.g. Theory 1
     └─ Module           e.g. Intervals
         └─ Assignment   e.g. "Seconds" — complete after 3 exercises

Section                  e.g. MUS201-002   (students see: Classroom)
 ├─ follows one Course   → gives students its modules
 ├─ its own additions    → modules and exercises for this section only
 ├─ its hides            → course work this section doesn't see
 ├─ Students             → joined with the school code, then the section code
 └─ You                  → its Section Teacher
```

## 3. Getting around

Everything starts from the **account menu** in the top-right corner of every page. It shows your
name, or your email address if you haven't set a name.

| Menu item | Takes you to |
|---|---|
| **Sections I Teach** | The sections you're assigned to. This is your main page. |
| **My Sections** | Sections you're enrolled in *as a participant*, plus the join-code forms. Most teachers won't need it. |
| **Manage School** | Your school's page: its join code and member list. |
| **Home** | The home page, including free practice (**Sandbox**). |
| **My Progress** | Your own practice history. |
| **View as** | Preview the site as a student. See [section 11](#11-seeing-the-site-as-a-student). |
| **Log out** | |

Each page is titled to match the menu item or button that opens it.

## 4. Your sections

Open the account menu → **Sections I Teach**.

Each section assigned to you appears as a card showing:

- the **section name** — click it to open the roster
- its **Join code**
- how many students have joined
- **View roster**, **Curriculum** and **Edit** buttons

**No cards?** No section has been assigned to you yet. Ask your School Admin to assign you — they do it
from the section's Edit page.

---

## 5. The roster

Click a section's name, or **View roster** on its card. The page is titled *<section> — Roster*.

At the top you'll see the **Section code** and the **School code**, with a reminder that new students
join the school with the school code first, then the section with the section code. There are also
**Curriculum** and **Edit Section** buttons, and the number of students.

Below that is one row per student:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Student** | The student's name, or their email if they haven't set one |
| **Melodic** | Average score over the student's 20 most recent melodic exercises |
| **Rhythmic** | Average rhythm accuracy over their 20 most recent rhythmic exercises |
| **Harmonic** | Average score over their 20 most recent harmonic exercises |
| **Holistic** | Average score over their 20 most recent holistic exercises |

A dash (**—**) means the student hasn't made any exercises in that mode yet.

> **Good to know.** These averages include *everything* the student has practised in that mode —
> free practice and other classes too — not only this section's assignments. The roster doesn't yet
> show which assignments each student has completed.

If nobody has joined yet, you'll see *"No students have joined yet. Share the join code: …"*

### Removing a student from your section

1. On the roster, click **Remove** on the student's row.
2. A confirmation page explains: *"The student can rejoin using the section join code if needed.
   Their exercise history will not be deleted."*
3. Click **Remove Student**, or **Cancel** to back out.

This takes the student out of this section only. They stay in the school, and all their practice
history is kept.

## 6. Getting students into your section

Students join in three steps, and **the order matters**:

1. **Create an account** on the site's **Register** page.
2. **Join the school.** Account menu → **My Classrooms** → under **Join a school**, enter the
   **school join code** → **Join School**.
3. **Join the section.** On the same page, under **Join a classroom**, enter the **section join code**
   → **Join**.

**Both codes are at the top of your section's roster.** The school code is also on your school page
(**Manage School**). Hand out both codes together.

If a student tries the section code before joining the school, they'll see *"You must join school
"…" first. Ask your teacher for the school join code."*

## 7. Your section's curriculum

Click **Curriculum** on a section's card or roster. The page is titled *<section> — Curriculum*.

If the section has no course yet, the page says so: a School Admin has to assign one before you can
build on it.

### The module list

Every module your students see is listed in order, in a table:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **#** | The module's order — students see modules sorted by this number, lowest first |
| **Module** | Its name — click it to open the module's page |
| **Source** | **From course** — it comes from the course, shared with every section following it. **This Section only** — you added it for this section. **Hidden** — you've hidden it from this section. |
| **Assignments** | How many assignments this section sees in it |

### Hiding course modules

A course module can't be deleted from here — it belongs to the course — but you can hide it from your
section:

- **Hide module** takes it out of your students' module list. They can't open it or its exercises, even
  with a direct link.
- **Restore module** brings it back.

Hiding never deletes anything. Students' progress on hidden work is kept, and reappears when you
restore it.

### Adding a module for this section only

At the bottom of the page:

1. Type a **Module name**.
2. Type an **Order** number. Pick a number that places it where you want among the course's modules.
3. Press **Add**.

The new module appears in the list as **This Section only**. Click it to add exercises
([section 8](#8-adding-exercises)).

### Deleting a module you added

Press **Delete** on one of your own modules and confirm *"Delete module "…" and all its exercises?
This cannot be undone."* This deletes the module, its exercises, and students' progress on them. Course
modules have no Delete button — hide them instead.

> **If your School Admin changes your section's course,** the modules and exercises you added for the
> old course are set aside — your students won't see them. They come back if the section is switched
> back to that course.

## 8. Adding exercises

Click a module's name on the Curriculum page. The page is titled *<section> — <module>*.

### What's on the page

- **Course assignments** — the course's assignments in this module, with **Hide** and **Restore**. Hiding
  works like hiding a module: your students stop seeing it, and nothing is deleted.
- **This Section's own assignments** — assignments you added, with **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Remove**.
- **Add Assignment** — the form for adding a new one.

On a module you added yourself, only your own assignments and the form appear.

### How exercises work

There are two kinds, and they behave differently:

- **Melodic, Rhythmic and Harmonic** assignments describe a *kind* of exercise using filters —
  difficulty, time signature, topic and so on. **Each time a student starts one, the site picks a
  random exercise from the library that matches your filters.** Students get variety, and repeat
  exercises give different material.
- **Holistic** assignments point at **one specific exercise** that you choose. Every student gets that
  same exercise.

### Adding an exercise

1. Choose the **Type**: Melodic, Rhythmic, Harmonic or Holistic.
2. Give it a **Name** — this is what students see, such as *Basic Melodic Dictation*.
3. Set the **Order** — its position within the module, lowest first.
4. Choose a **Completion** rule (below).
5. **Holistic:** choose the exercise from the **Exercise** list.
   **Melodic, Rhythmic, Harmonic:** set the filters (below).
6. Check the **live match count**, which shows how many library exercises your filters currently
   select. Make sure it's above zero.
7. Press **Add Assignment**.

The exercise appears for your section's students straight away, and for no one else.

### Completion rules

| Option | A student completes it when… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **By number of exercises** | they've submitted it this many times, whatever they scored | *3 exercise(s) to complete* |
| **By passing score** | they've submitted this many times at or above the minimum score | *2 pass(es) at 80 % minimum score* |

The defaults are 1 exercise, or 1 pass at 70 %. The site records each student's best score either way.
Students see a check mark and their best score once they've completed it, and a progress bar whenever
more than one exercise or pass is required.

### Filters

Filters appear for Melodic, Rhythmic and Harmonic exercises. **Leave a filter empty to allow
anything.**

| Filter | Used for | Ticking several boxes means |
|---|---|---|
| **Difficulty** — 1 to 5 stars | All three | any of them |
| **Time Signature** | Melodic, Rhythmic | any of them |
| **Shortest Note Value** | Melodic, Rhythmic | any of them |
| **Clef** | Melodic | any of them |
| **Category** — Diatonic, Chromatic, Mode Mixture, Modal | Harmonic | any of them |
| **Key Signature** — type one key, such as *C*, *G* or *Bb* | Harmonic | — one key only |
| **Topics** | All three | an exercise must have **all** of them |

Only the topics that exist for the chosen type are offered.

If your filters match nothing, a student who starts the exercise sees *"No exercises match the filters
for this assignment. Ask your teacher to adjust the filters."* and can't complete it. Loosen the
filters — usually Topics or Key Signature are the culprits. The live match count warns you before it
happens.

### Editing, duplicating and removing your exercises

- **Edit** opens a panel with the same fields. For Holistic exercises you can change the name, order and
  completion rule, but not which exercise it is — remove it and add it again.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy you can then edit. Handy for *"the same thing, but harder"*.
- **Remove** deletes it, and your students' progress on it.

You can only edit, duplicate or remove your section's own assignments. The course's assignments are shared
with other sections, so here you can only hide them.

## 9. Section settings

Click **Edit** on a section's card, or **Edit Section** on its roster.

### Section Settings

You can change the **Section Name** and press **Save Changes**. The **Course** is shown but can't be
changed here — *"Ask your School Admin to change it."*

### Curriculum

A short card with **Curriculum →**, which opens the curriculum page ([section 7](#7-your-sections-curriculum)).

Assigning teachers and deleting sections are School Admin tasks, so those controls don't appear for
you.

## 10. Your school page

Open the account menu → **Manage School**. The page is titled *<school> — Manage School*.

It shows:

- **Your role here** — *Section Teacher*
- the **School Join Code** — hand this out along with each section's code. Only School Admins can
  regenerate it.
- the **Members** table — each member's email, name, role and the date they joined

### Removing someone from the school

You can remove members whose role is below yours — in practice, students.

1. Click **Remove** on their row.
2. Confirm. The dialog warns that they *"will also be dropped from this school's sections."*

Removing someone from the school takes them out of **every section in that school**, not just yours.
Their practice history is kept, and they could rejoin later with the school join code.

To add people or change roles, ask your School Admin.

## 11. Seeing the site as a student

Open the account menu → **View as** → **Student**.

The site now behaves exactly as it would for a student: the menu changes, and any page a student
couldn't open is blocked for you too. A banner across the top of every page reads *"Viewing as
Student. Pages beyond this role are blocked, exactly as they would be for a real student."*

To return, click **Back to Section Teacher** in that banner. The banner stays visible on every page,
so you can always get back.

## 12. When something goes wrong

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A **Forbidden** page — *"You don't have the permission to access the requested resource."* | That page is for School Admins, or for a section you don't teach | Go back. Ask your School Admin if it needs doing. |
| A student reports *"No exercises match the filters for this assignment. Ask your teacher to adjust the filters."* | That exercise's filters match nothing in the library | If it's one of your section's own exercises, loosen its filters ([section 8](#8-adding-exercises)). If it's a course exercise, tell your School Admin — or hide it for your section. |
| Students suddenly see no modules | The section's course was removed or changed | Ask your School Admin to set the course again. Progress isn't deleted. |
| A module or exercise you hid still appears when you preview as a student | You're looking at a different section, or haven't restored it | Check the *Source* column on that section's Curriculum page. |
| A student reports *"You must join school "…" first."* | They skipped joining the school | Give them the school code from your roster. |
| A student reports *"Invalid join code."* or *"Invalid school join code."* | A typo, or the code has been changed | Check both codes at the top of your roster. |
| *"You can only remove members below your own role."* | You tried to remove another teacher or an admin | Ask your School Admin. |
| A section is missing from **Sections I Teach** | You aren't assigned to it | Ask your School Admin to assign you. |
