## 1. Your role at a glance

You're a **Section Teacher**. A School Admin has assigned you to teach one or more sections, and you
look after the students in them day to day. Your School Admin builds the courses and creates the
sections; you work with what they've set up.

| You can | Ask your School Admin to |
|---|---|
| See every section assigned to you | Create or delete a section |
| Open a section's roster and see students' average scores | Rename a section, or change its course |
| See and share a section's join code | Assign a teacher to a section |
| Remove a student from your section | Build or change courses, modules and exercises |
| Remove a student from your school | Add people to the school, or change anyone's role |
| Preview the site as a student | Give you the school's join code |

**How you got this role.** Your School Admin gave you the Section Teacher role in your school, and
your account picked it up automatically. If that role is ever removed, your account goes back to
being a student account.

## 2. Key ideas

**School.** Your organisation. People join it, and it owns the courses.

**Course.** A curriculum, such as *Theory 1*. A course is made of modules.

**Module.** A unit within a course, such as *Intervals*. Modules appear in a fixed order.

**Module exercise.** One assignment inside a module. Each has a completion rule — either a number of
attempts, or a number of passes at a minimum score.

**Section.** A group of students taught together, usually named like a class section, such as
*MUS201-002*. **Your students see the word "Classroom" instead of "Section" — it's the same thing.**
Each section follows one course, and that course is what gives its students their modules.

**Join codes.** Short codes students type to join. There are two — a **school join code** and a
**section join code** — and students need both, in that order.

**Exercise types.** Melodic, Rhythmic, Harmonic and Holistic dictation.

How the pieces fit together:

```
School
 └─ Course               e.g. Theory 1
     └─ Module           e.g. Intervals
         └─ Module exercise   e.g. "Seconds" — complete after 3 attempts

Section                  e.g. MUS201-002   (students see: Classroom)
 ├─ follows one Course   → gives students its modules
 ├─ Students             → joined with the section join code
 └─ You                  → its Section Teacher
```

## 3. Getting around

Everything starts from the **account menu** in the top-right corner of every page. It shows your
name, or your email address if you haven't set a name.

| Menu item | Takes you to |
|---|---|
| **Sections I Teach** | The sections you're assigned to. This is your main page. |
| **My Sections** | Sections you're enrolled in *as a participant*, plus the join-code forms. Most teachers won't need it. |
| **Manage School** | Your school's page and its member list. |
| **Home** | The home page, including free practice (**Sandbox**). |
| **My Progress** | Your own practice history. |
| **View as** | Preview the site as a student. See [section 9](#9-seeing-the-site-as-a-student). |
| **Log out** | |

> **Heads-up.** The page you reach from **Sections I Teach** is titled *My Sections* at the top — and
> so is the different page you reach from **My Sections**. If you can see section cards with
> **View roster** buttons, you're in the right place.

## 4. Your sections

Open the account menu → **Sections I Teach**.

Each section assigned to you appears as a card showing:

- the **section name** — click it to open the roster
- its **Join code**
- how many students have joined
- **View roster** and **Edit** buttons

**No cards?** No section has been assigned to you yet. Ask your School Admin to assign you — they do it
from the section's Edit page.

---

## 5. The roster

Click a section's name, or **View roster** on its card.

The top of the page shows the section's name, its **Join code**, an **Edit Section** button, and the
number of students. Below that is one row per student:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Student** | The student's name, or their email if they haven't set one |
| **Melodic** | Average score over the student's 20 most recent melodic attempts |
| **Rhythmic** | Average rhythm accuracy over their 20 most recent rhythmic attempts |
| **Harmonic** | Average score over their 20 most recent harmonic attempts |
| **Holistic** | Average score over their 20 most recent holistic attempts |

A dash (**—**) means the student hasn't made any attempts in that mode yet.

> **Good to know.** These averages include *everything* the student has practised in that mode —
> free practice and other classes too — not only this section's assignments. The roster doesn't yet
> show which module exercises each student has completed.

If nobody has joined yet, you'll see *"No students have joined yet. Share the join code: …"*

### Removing a student from your section

1. On the roster, click **Remove** on the student's row.
2. A confirmation page explains: *"The student can rejoin using the section join code if needed.
   Their attempt history will not be deleted."*
3. Click **Remove Student**, or **Cancel** to back out.

This takes the student out of this section only. They stay in the school, and all their practice
history is kept.

## 6. Getting students into your section

Students join in three steps, and **the order matters**:

1. **Create an account** on the site's **Register** page.
2. **Join the school.** Account menu → **My Classrooms** → under **Join a school**, enter the
   **school join code** → **Join School**.
3. **Join the section.** On the same page, under **Join a classroom**, enter the **section join code**
   → **Join**.

**Where to find the codes:**

- **Section join code** — on the section's card, and at the top of its roster. Share it freely.
- **School join code** — you can't see this one; only School Admins can. Get it from your School Admin
  and hand it out together with the section code.

If a student tries the section code before joining the school, they'll see *"You must join school
"…" first. Ask your teacher for the school join code."* — so it's worth having the school code on hand
before class.

## 7. Section settings

Click **Edit** on a section's card, or **Edit Section** on its roster.

> **⚠️ Don't press Save Changes on this page.**
>
> Because of a known issue, saving the **Section Settings** card as a Section Teacher **removes the
> section's course** — even if you only meant to change the name. When that happens, every student in
> the section loses access to its modules.
>
> If you need a section renamed, ask your School Admin.
>
> **If it's already happened:** ask your School Admin to open the section's Edit page, choose the
> course again under **Course**, and press **Save Changes**. Students' progress isn't deleted, so
> reconnecting the course brings everything back.

The Edit page has three parts.

### Section Settings

The section's **Section Name** and **Course**. See the warning above.

### Assign Section Teacher

This card is for School Admins. You can see the form, but submitting it leads to a **Forbidden**
error page. The same goes for the **Delete Section** link at the bottom of the page.

### Section-Specific Overrides

Lists each module in the section's course, with its exercises. There are **Hide module** and **Hide**
buttons, and **Restore** to undo.

> **⚠️ Hiding doesn't currently affect students.**
>
> Pressing **Hide** or **Hide module** shows a success message and marks the item *Hidden* on this
> page — but students still see those exercises and can still complete them. Until this is fixed,
> don't rely on hiding to keep work away from students. Ask your School Admin to change the course
> instead.

## 8. Your school page

Open the account menu → **Manage School**.

The page shows your school's name, **Your role here**, and a **Members** table listing each member's
email, name, role and the date they joined.

Roles on this page appear under the site's internal names:

| Shown as | Means |
|---|---|
| `student` | Student |
| `class_teacher` | Section Teacher — you |
| `admin_teacher` | School Admin |

### Removing someone from the school

You can remove members whose role is below yours — in practice, students.

1. Click **Remove** on their row.
2. Confirm. The dialog warns that they *"will also be dropped from this school's sections."*

Removing someone from the school takes them out of **every section in that school**, not just yours.
Their practice history is kept, and they could rejoin later with the school join code.

To add people, change roles, or see the school join code, ask your School Admin.

## 9. Seeing the site as a student

Open the account menu → **View as** → **Student**.

The site now behaves exactly as it would for a student: the menu changes, and any page a student
couldn't open is blocked for you too. A banner across the top of every page reads *"Viewing as
Student. Pages beyond this role are blocked, exactly as they would be for a real student."*

To return, click **Back to Section Teacher** in that banner. The banner stays visible on every page,
so you can always get back.

## 10. When something goes wrong

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A **Forbidden** page — *"You don't have the permission to access the requested resource."* | That page or button is for School Admins, or for a section you don't teach | Go back. Ask your School Admin if it needs doing. |
| Students suddenly see no modules in your section | The section's course was removed — usually by saving Section Settings ([section 7](#7-section-settings)) | Ask your School Admin to reconnect the course. Progress is kept. |
| A student reports *"No exercises match the filters for this module exercise. Ask your teacher to adjust the filters."* | That exercise's settings don't match anything in the library | Tell your School Admin which module and exercise. |
| A student reports *"You must join school "…" first."* | They skipped joining the school | Give them the school join code (get it from your School Admin). |
| A student reports *"Invalid join code."* or *"Invalid school join code."* | A typo, or the code has been changed | Re-check the section code on its card. Ask your School Admin for the current school code. |
| *"You can only remove members below your own role."* | You tried to remove another teacher or an admin | Ask your School Admin. |
| A section is missing from **Sections I Teach** | You aren't assigned to it | Ask your School Admin to assign you. |

## 11. Known issues

These are problems in the current version of the site. They're being tracked, and this guide will be
updated as they're fixed.

- **Saving Section Settings removes the section's course.** Don't press **Save Changes** on a
  section's Edit page. See [section 7](#7-section-settings).
- **Hide and Hide module don't hide anything from students.** See [section 7](#7-section-settings).
- **School Admin controls appear on your Edit page.** The **Assign** form and the **Delete Section**
  link are visible to you but lead to a Forbidden page.
- **Students are told to ask their teacher for the school join code**, but Section Teachers can't see
  it. Keep a copy from your School Admin.
- **Internal role names** such as `class_teacher` appear on the school page.
- **Two different pages are both titled "My Sections."** See [section 3](#3-getting-around).
